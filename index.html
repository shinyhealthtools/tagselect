About TagSelect
A browser-based sampling and randomization tool for researchers. No data leaves your device.

Simple Random Sampling (SRS)

Every unit in the population has equal probability π = n/N of selection. A Fisher-Yates shuffle is applied to indices; the first n are selected. Sampling is performed without replacement.

Systematic Sampling

The sampling interval k = ⌊N/n⌋ is computed. A random start r is drawn from the discrete uniform distribution U(1, k). Units at positions r, r+k, r+2k, … form the sample.

Proportional Stratified Sampling

The population is partitioned into H strata. Within stratum h, the allocation is nₕ = round(n × Nₕ/N). SRS without replacement is applied within each stratum.

Equal Stratified Sampling

Each of the H strata receives nₕ = round(n/H) units, regardless of stratum size. Useful when all strata warrant equal representation in the analysis.

Complete (Balanced) Randomization

Exactly ⌊N/arms⌋ participants are assigned to each arm; remainder units are assigned round-robin. The full assignment vector is then globally shuffled, ensuring balance.

Block Randomization

Participants are grouped into blocks of size b (rounded to nearest multiple of arms). Within each block, arm assignments are randomly permuted. Guarantees balance at every block boundary.

Stratified Randomization

Complete or block randomization is applied independently within each stratum. Combines stratification with experimental assignment for covariate-adaptive balance.

Privacy & data handling

All computations happen in your browser. No data is uploaded, transmitted, or stored on any server. The random number generator uses a seeded linear congruential PRNG — specify a seed to make results reproducible.
