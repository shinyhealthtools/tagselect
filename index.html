About TagSelect
A browser-based sampling and randomization tool for researchers. All computations happen locally — no data ever leaves your device.

🎯Simple Random Sampling (SRS)

Every unit in the population has equal probability π = n/N of selection. A Fisher-Yates shuffle is applied to indices; the first n are selected. Sampling is performed without replacement using CSPRNG (crypto.getRandomValues).

📐Systematic Sampling

The fractional interval k = N/n is computed. A random start r is drawn from U(0, k). Units at positions ⌊r⌋, ⌊r+k⌋, ⌊r+2k⌋, … form the sample, guaranteeing exactly n samples with even spacing.

🗂️Proportional Stratified

The population is partitioned into H strata. Allocation nₕ = ⌊n × Nₕ/N⌋ is computed per stratum, with the largest-remainder method distributing residuals to ensure Σnₕ = n exactly. No forced minimum — strata may receive 0 if quota rounds to zero.

⚖️Equal Stratified

Each of the H strata receives nₕ = ⌊n/H⌋ units via largest-remainder, ensuring the total is exactly n. Useful when all strata warrant equal representation regardless of size.

✅Complete (Balanced)

Exactly ⌊N/arms⌋ participants are assigned to each arm; remainder units are assigned round-robin. The full assignment vector is then globally shuffled, ensuring balance.

🧩Block Randomization

Block size must be a strict multiple of number of arms. Within each block, arm assignments are randomly permuted. Guarantees balance at every block boundary. Invalid block sizes are rejected with an error.

🔬Stratified Randomization

Complete or block randomization is applied independently within each stratum — the gold standard in RCTs. Block-within-strata option available for covariate-adaptive balance.

🔐Privacy & Randomness

All computations happen in your browser. No data is uploaded or stored. Without a seed, crypto.getRandomValues provides cryptographically secure randomness. With a seed, a deterministic LCG is used for exact reproducibility.
